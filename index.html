Digital Archaeological Infrastructure

About ArchaeoMap

ArchaeoMap is a GIS-based digital archaeological field survey and spatial analysis system designed to bring artifact documentation, geographic visualization and preliminary spatial analysis into a unified digital environment.

Why ArchaeoMap?

Archaeological field data can become difficult to manage when information, photographs and geographic locations are maintained separately in disconnected field notebooks, paper context sheets, and standalone spreadsheets.

ArchaeoMap connects these components into one cohesive system: tying artifact typologies and photographic evidence directly to geographic coordinates, stratigraphic horizons, and automated spatial clustering.
